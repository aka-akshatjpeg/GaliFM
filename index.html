About

More Than Just Music

Gali FM brings the atmosphere of everyday life into your listening experience. Inspired by India's streets, neighbourhoods, memories, and culture, it creates a place to slow down, find your mood, and enjoy the moments that make an ordinary day feel special.

Each station is a place, not a playlist. A night highway, a warm kitchen, a construction site at noon, a living room with an old cassette player. The music and atmosphere belong together.

Pick a world. Press play. Leave it on while you work, cook, or rest. That's all there is to it.

What you get

What you can do on Gali FM

Seven Radio Worlds

Browse seven distinctly themed ambient stations — each with its own atmosphere, character, and music mood. Switch freely between them at any time.

A Built-in Music Player

A clean, accessible audio player with play, pause, prev, next, and volume controls. Supports both individual tracks and continuous streams, with honest loading and error states.

An Indian Atmosphere

Designed around the visual culture and everyday character of India. Each station carries its own story: a neighbourhood barbershop, a late-night bus ride, a kitchen after dinner.

Made for Everyday Moments

Use Gali FM while working, studying, cooking, or winding down. No algorithm. No recommendations. Just the right sound for the moment you're in.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Gali FM?

Gali FM is an ambient radio project with seven themed stations inspired by everyday Indian life — a barbershop, a night bus, a kitchen after dinner, a construction site at noon, a living room, a Bhojpuri celebration, and a late-office evening. Each station has its own atmosphere, music, and character.

How do I listen?

Click Start Listening or Listen now to go to the Stations page. Select a world, then use the player's play button. No account, no sign-up required.

Is Gali FM free to use?

Yes. Gali FM is completely free. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, and no account required to listen.

Can I listen while working or studying?

Absolutely — that's exactly what it's designed for. Start a station, minimise the tab, and let it run. The audio keeps playing in the background.

Why isn't there any audio playing yet?

Gali FM's player is built and ready. Audio will play once licensed track URLs are connected in the project. The player architecture handles loading, streaming, errors, and track changes — it just needs content.

How can I get in touch?

Contact and support details will appear here once they are set up by the project owner. In the meantime, refer to the Privacy and Contact links in the footer.
